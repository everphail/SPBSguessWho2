First-time setup (site owner only)

To enable multiplayer, open firebase-config.js and paste the Firebase web app configuration. Enable Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database, then apply the database rules in database.rules.json. Publish the files together on GitHub Pages. Players will not need Firebase accounts.

Waiting for a second player…

Ask your opponent a yes/no question aloud or through your own call/chat. Tap faces to eliminate them from your personal board. Your opponent cannot see your eliminations.

How to play: Each player gets a secret person. Take turns asking yes/no questions (for example, “Is your person wearing glasses?”). Tap the people you rule out. When you think you know the answer, make a final guess. Your opponent confirms whether your final guess is correct. A correct guess wins; a wrong guess loses. You can talk over WhatsApp or any voice call while playing.
