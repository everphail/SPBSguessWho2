Type a yes/no question in your own words. The computer understands a limited set of visible features; it will tell you when it cannot answer.

Think you know the AI's person? Make your final guess.

Easy asks less efficient questions; Smart usually chooses good questions; Smartest plans two questions ahead. All levels answer your questions using the same character data. AI mode is free, runs locally, and does not send questions to a language-model service. Answers are based on manually assigned visible traits and may occasionally be inaccurate.

How to play: Each player gets a secret person. Take turns asking yes/no questions (for example, “Is your person wearing glasses?”). Tap the people you rule out. When you think you know the answer, make a final guess. Your opponent confirms whether your final guess is correct. Turns switch automatically after each answer. A correct guess wins; a wrong guess loses. Use the built-in question box and Yes/No buttons to play entirely on this website.
